Why do your posts get ignored? It's not the algorithm. Most posts open with the offer, and readers scroll straight past it. Here's the fix in three steps. First, name the problem in your reader's own words. Second, give them one idea they haven't heard before. Third, invite a small next step, like a reply or a question. Do this for thirty days and watch what happens. Clear beats clever, every single time. Save this, and try it on your next post.
